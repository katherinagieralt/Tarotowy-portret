## Archetype and General Meaning

After the heavy and exhausting cut of Death, the soul lands in the soothing hands of the Angel of Temperance. This is the card of healing, the vibration of flow, and above all, alchemy, combining Fire with Water, body with soul, reason with intuition. Temperance teaches us the art of maintaining the right proportions in everything we do. It is calm mediation and the soothing of internal wounds. It says that nothing is achieved by sudden bursts (like in the Chariot), but only by gentle evolution, systematic mixing, until the process reaches the golden ratio and perfect balance.

## Psychology and Inner World

You belong to a group of souls who bring healing simply through their presence. You are a harmonious person with an exceptionally stable psyche, "connected and grounded on earth, yet with wings in the clouds." You know what compromise is. You rarely let yourself be carried away by extreme emotions for long. Inside you, there is a quiet river of peace. You don't have to prove anything to the world; your center of gravity is always perfectly controlled, and you consider extremes to be glitches in the human system.

## Potential

Your greatest gift is interpersonal and psychic alchemy, and the ability to harmonize the space around you.
*   **The gift of healing and psychology:** You possess a unique talent in professions related to health (medicine, herbalism, caregiving, alternative medicine).
*   **Synergy and balance:** You can take two conflicting ideas/people/departments and forge a compromise from which a fantastic, new project hatches (the alchemy of collaboration).
*   **Stress-free goal achievement:** You move slowly but purposefully, reaching the summit with a smile and without burnout.

## Shadow

The unintegrated energy of Temperance manifests, of course, as a complete lack of measure (lack of balance). In the shadow, you are like a broken faucet, you throw yourself into eating compulsions, shopping binges, or workaholism, unable to see the "golden mean." On the other hand, it can be such a huge resistance to any conflict that you prefer to drink lukewarm water and live a gray life, devoid of distinct flavor, avoiding absolutely all emotional fluctuations. Compromise turns into rotten conformism, and "fluidity" transforms into psychological stagnation.

## Relationships and Emotions

In relationships, you primarily need stabilization, partnership, and a mutual, patient smoothing of your sharp edges. Explosive, sweep-you-off-your-feet romances will, in the long run, be treated by you as a disturbance of your beloved living space. You are like clean water that dissolves a partner's anger. Love for Temperance is a relationship between friends who can sit by the fireplace for years and never get bored, understanding each other without words, yet they don't lack vital fire, as long as it is dosed with love.

## Symbolism

*   **Angel:** A supernatural being bringing healing; the personification of androgynous balance (lack of distinct gender), a combination of Yin and Yang traits.
*   **Two cups:** Mixing water (hot with cold). This is the continuous process of life. A symbol of pouring knowledge from the subconscious to the conscious.
*   **One foot on land, the other in water:** Another balance. The foot on land is grounded in material reality (reason), while the foot in water communes with the collective unconscious and emotions (intuition).
*   **A path leading to a crown-shaped sun in the background:** The evolutionary process of returning to enlightenment after removing toxins (Death), a gentle climb towards the crown of the tree of life.

## Example of Action in Life

Imagine a stressful job in an advertising agency. Two key bosses are tearing each other apart over what direction a multi-million-dollar campaign should take (one wants fire and aggression, the other conservative classicism). A person with the vibration of Temperance steps in, takes a bit of madness from one, a bit of form from the other, and mixes it so perfectly that they create something brilliant, and both gentlemen shake hands. A soothing mixture of emotions and proportions was introduced, resolving the stalemate without using a single word of criticism.
